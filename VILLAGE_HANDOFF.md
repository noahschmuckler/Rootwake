## V0: presence
The question: do figures going in and out of houses on a day's rhythm already read as people living there?
- **The place.** A meadow by a stream. Six round houses on a ring nine metres from the green, doors to the green, thatch cones, a window each. The fire at the green's centre, worn paths from it to every door and out through every gap between houses to the places they keep to: a berry thicket, the stream's bank, a copse, a field of tilled strips, a goat pen with three goats, a standing stone. A wood round the edge.
- **The eight.** Marlo and Tansy (one house), Pip, Wren, Bram and Hazel (one house), Odo, Nell. Each keeps to a place (Odo keeps the fire: the elder to be; Nell the stone: the spiritual leader to be; two to the thicket, one each to the copse, stream, field and pen), rises at their own minute after dawn, and walks at their own pace. Names over their heads while near; a thought bubble when the rhythm moves them ("picking berries", "to the fire", "home") and now and then at the fire ("the stream is low").
- **The rhythm.** A day is 1440 ticks, 20 real minutes; tick 0 is 06:00. Dawn to 07:30 (each leaves at their rising minute), morning at their place, noon 11:30 to 13:00 together at the fire facing it and talking, afternoon at their place, dusk from 18:00 (each goes home at half their rising minute after), night from 19:30 indoors. At a place they stand and now and then take a step to a new spot. Paths go by the green, never through the ring.
- **Only while watched.** Ticks come from real seconds while the page is open; hidden, nothing passes. The village saves every five seconds and on hide (`rootwake-village-v1`).
- **Bodies.** The hobbits are Hulda's skeleton at 0.46 m (`createHulda` takes `FigureOptions`: height, colours, no vine locks, no leaves, a cloth hem), so the same Mixamo clips drive all nine; a hobbit's stride is fed the speed it would be at Hulda's size. The shown figure eases after the model's tick so a tick's step reads as walking.
- **Light.** The sun by the clock; sky through dusk to a night blue; the fire and the windows lit at night.
- **Hulda** walks in the free-flow controls, third person, first person by the button. They do not see her. No sinking, no trunks here yet.

## V0.1: her ways, thoughts always, facing and fidgeting (Noah's first phone notes)
- **Her ways through the meadow** (the clearing's moves, ported): press into any tree to enter its trunk, rise to the crown, slide round it and leap to a crown within reach; double tap the stick and she is a bulge under the grass (`GRASS_SPEED` 3.8 m/s, faster than her run, free in any direction, not under the houses or the water); run along one of the tree roots that join the copse and the wood and it takes her (`ROOT_SPEED` 6.5 m/s, held to its path, the aligned root at a tree, back to reverse, sideways for a quarter second to drop into the grass); pushing down at a trunk's foot sinks her into the grass; a double tap of the stick brings her out of any form. The meadow goes glassy while she is under it, so the fast lanes show.
- **Thoughts always.** A hobbit out of doors always has one: what they are doing ("gathering berries", "milking the goats", "keeping the fire"), where they are going ("walking to the thicket", "walking to the fire", "going home"), talk by the fire, and the chatter for a few ticks when it comes.
- **Facing.** They walked backwards because the rig faces −Z at yaw 0 and the model's heading is an angle in x,z: yaw = −π/2 − heading. Fixed and checked in the journey (the share of walking frames that face forward).
- **At a place** they turn to face one thing and then another every few ticks and step to a new spot twice as often as before, instead of standing still.

## V0.2: one camera rule, the double tap on the stick, a paced walk (Noah's second phone notes)
- **One camera rule for every form.** Every form's camera (grass, root, trunk, crown, leap) is now the shared third person's rule (`orbitCamera`: behind her by yaw, a fixed lift, the look tilted by pitch), so the same drag turns the view the same way whether she is the walker, the bulge or the knot. Before, the still forms lifted the camera by pitch and looked at her, so a drag read inverted between forms. The same rule is in the karst free flow and the clearing.
- **The double tap lives on the stick only.** On the ground a double tap of the stick takes her under the grass where she stands (the clearing: into the roots; the karst: into the nearest plant's roots within reach); in any other form it brings her out. The screen's double tap is gone; screen taps only walk and look. Pressing into a tree or plant still enters it.
- **A root catches her along the whole step.** Under the grass the root check runs along the distance she moved this frame (`ROOT_CATCH_STEP`), for any root within `ROOT_CATCH` whose run agrees with hers (`alignedRoot` in the model, the best aligned first, not only the nearest), so a slow frame cannot carry her over a root and a neighbouring root cannot hide the one she is running along. The browser journey aims her along a copse root rather than spiralling.
- **Hobbits walk at their pace.** The shown figure used to ease toward the model's tick position at 1.35 × pace + 0.2 m/s, so each tick was a dash and a wait. Now it walks at 1.08 × the hobbit's own pace (only a jump of more than 2.5 m, a hitch, closes at 2.5 ×), so a destination once set is one continuous walk; the small extra keeps the lag from growing over a route.
- **Two clocks.** The village's ticks now come from the real seconds between frames (`WALL_CAP` 2 s drops a stall), while the animation step stays capped at 0.05 s; before, a slow renderer shrank real seconds into fewer ticks (GitHub's Chromium counted one tick in three seconds), which would also have slowed the day on a phone dropping below 20 fps.
- Only the village workflow runs on this branch; the karst free flow and the clearing carry the same camera and stick changes in source (`src/karstFlow.ts`, `src/flow.ts`, their intros and journeys) but their sites republish only from their own branches.

## V1: hunger and the land (Noah's brief: needs, stores, gathering, a steady state)
The question: is the village legible as a system, and can what it takes from the land be read off the land?
- **The land gives by the day** (`villageModel.ts`, tuning beside each): berries regrow on the seven bushes at `BERRY_REGROW` 12 a day up to `BERRY_CAP` 35; `BRANCHES_PER_DAY` 6 drop under the copse at dawn (up to 12 lying); the goats have `MILK_PER_DAY` 6 at dawn and no more; the five strips each ripen over `CROP_DAYS` 4 (staggered, so one comes ripe most days) and give `GRAIN_PER_STRIP` 6; the stream is endless. All of it is shown by count: berries on the bushes come and go, sticks lie and are gone, stalks rise green to gold and are cut, pails stand by the pen gate.
- **Stores on the green's edge**, each on the side of its place, in the open: a rack of baskets (cap 8), the trough (10), the woodpile (12), the bin of sacks (12), the shelf of pails (8). Each shows its count as things. The caps bound the take: a gatherer whose store is full stops picking, so left alone the village never strips the land.
- **Gathering is seen.** At their place a hobbit takes one unit every `GATHER_TICKS` 12 while the land has one and the store has room (what others already carry counts against the room); an armful (`CARRY`: 6 berries, 3 pails, 6 grain, 3 sticks, 2 buckets) or the last of what there is goes to the store in the hand (a basket, bucket, bundle, sack or pail on the right hand bone, sized in the skeleton's centimetres), is handed over, and they walk back. Wren harvests a strip only when it is ripe and the bin has room; Bram milks what the goats have; Marlo picks up sticks; Pip fills buckets.
- **Two meals a day** from whichever food store is fullest by share of its cap (so the diet spreads over berries, milk and grain and no one store is eaten down and re-picked while another waits full): the noon meal at the fire with a drink from the trough, each hobbit sitting `EAT_TICKS` 14 with "eating"; supper fetched from the store on the way home and carried in ("fetching supper", "home with supper"). Empty stores mean "nothing to eat" and a hungry night; nobody dies.
- **Hunger** climbs from 0 to 1 in `HUNGER_DAYS` 0.6 of a day (half as fast asleep) and falls to 0 at each meal; a small bar under the name shows it, green to amber to red, and the thought says "hungry" past 0.85. Left alone it peaks about 0.8 before supper.
- **The fire.** At `FIRE_WOOD_TICK` 690 (a little before dusk) Odo walks to the woodpile, carries `WOOD_PER_NIGHT` 5 to the fire and lays it; the fire burns it down through the night, and its flames and light are as big as the wood on it: embers only when none was laid.
- **The balance** (`balance(v)`): the last whole day's take of berries, wood and milk over what the land regrows (12 + 6 + 6). Under 1 the land gains. The stores fill in the first hours of the morning and after the noon meal, so the gatherers stand idle at their places much of the day: the take is bounded by the caps, which is the steady state. Left alone the village settles by day three at about 6 berries, 5 milk, 6 grain, 5 wood and 8 water a day, balance about 0.7, the thicket full, seven sticks lying, one pail unmilked. The eight-day test in `tests/village.test.ts` holds this.
- **Save** carries the stores, the land, the fire's wood, the takes, each hobbit's hunger, armful and errand (`rootwake-village-v1`, forgiving parse).

## V1.1: the clock in hand, and out is always possible (Noah's notes)
- **Dev control of time.** Tap the clock: an hour back or on, the next dawn / noon / dusk / night, and the day at ×1, ×8 or ×30. Forward jumps advance the model; a jump back replays the village from its seed to that tick, which is the same state, because the village is deterministic and she does not touch it yet. `?tick=N` opens the page at a tick; `__village.setTick`, `jumpTo`, `speed`. At speed the shown figures walk at that speed too.
- **Out of any form, anywhere.** She could not come out of a trunk or a root that had carried her into the wood: walking and emerging were bounded to the 44 m meadow, but the wood's trees and roots run out to 80 m, so the double tap was refused where she stood. The walkable world is now `WALK_RADIUS` (the meadow and the wood), and emerging looks round for the nearest ground she can stand on (not water, a house or a trunk) rather than refusing. The journey now double-taps out of a trunk in the wood and out of the grass there.

## W1: her hands, their prayer (Noah's decisions of 2026-09-23)
The question: does her helping read as a relationship, and does the village's turning to the stone read as thanks?
- **Three meals a day at the fire.** Breakfast on the way out at dawn (each at their rising time, an errand to the fire before their place), the noon meal with a drink from the trough, supper on the way home at dusk. Each meal is one unit of the fullest food by share; a hobbit eats each slot once (`mealSlot`). The village eats 24 a day.
- **Gathering paced to the next meal.** One unit every `GATHER_TICKS` 40 at the place (a session between meals yields about a meal's share of a gatherer's kind); armfuls `CARRY` 4 berries, 3 milk, 6 grain, 3 sticks, 2 buckets. The timer is set when a job begins, not on every step (a wander step used to reset it, which with the slower rate stopped all gathering: the first probe's deadlock).
- **Supplied, they pray.** Every `JOB_EVERY` 20 a gatherer asks `supplied`: food in the stores and on its way for `NEXT_MEAL_MARGIN` 2 meals (two, because nothing is gathered in the night, so supper must leave breakfast), water for everyone, wood for two nights. Supplied, the job flips to pray: they walk to the stone ("to the stone"), stand faced to it ("praying"); short again, they go back. Nell prays always and doubles the rate. Prayer pools at `PRAYER_PER_TICK` 0.012 per worshipper up to `PRAYER_CAP` 60, shown by the clock and as the stone's glow and light. Left alone it reaches the cap in about two days.
- **Her hands.** A ring at each yielding place (`STATIONS`, gather, 1.4 m, set on the green side of the place in the open, clear of the bushes, the trees, the strips and the fence), at each store (deliver, 1.1 m) and before the stone (shrine, 1.5 m); a ring brightens while she stands in it. Her collect and deliver clocks run on real time like the village's ticks (the capped animation step starved them on GitHub's renderer: two units in four seconds). Standing in a place's ring on her feet collects one unit every `COLLECT_S` 0.4 into her stack (one kind at a time, `STACK_CAP` 8: a meal for the village in about three seconds), from the same land and counted in the same take; in a store's ring one unit every `DELIVER_S` 0.12 goes in, under the cap. The stack is slabs on her back, coloured by kind. No sap, only time. A loaded stack refuses the grass and the trunks: the double tap wobbles the stack instead.
- **The miracle.** Before the stone the panel offers a spirit for each of the five jobs at `spiritCost` (`SPIRIT_COST` 20, times `SPIRIT_COST_RISE` 1.5 each time). A spirit is a small figure of leaves that runs the gather-and-carry loop at its place by day (`SPIRIT_PACE` 1.2, the same rate and armfuls, no meals, no prayer) and stands there by night; it shows a bundle while it carries. Its take counts against the room like anyone's, so a freed hobbit finds the store supplied and prays the more.
- **Steady state without her** (seeded 1, eight days): three meals each every day, no meal missed, hunger under 0.6, the thicket full, balance about 0.8, prayer at the cap; a spirit on the thicket lifts the praying share by about a third. The eight-day test holds this with a spirit on the stream.
- **Save** carries prayer, the spirits and her stack. `__village`: `stations`, `stack`, `prayer` (settable), `spirits`, `summon(keeps)`, `spiritCost()`, `praying()`.
- **Not yet, by Noah's call for this phase:** births (excess for long enough spawns a child whose needs come before its hands), the tree of miracles, the rival. Match-3 stays out.

## Honest limits
No hunting, no roles chosen, no personality: V2's. Crafting has no use for wood beyond the fire yet, so the woodpile only feeds the fire. They walk straight lines between waypoints and ignore each other on the way. The stream is a ribbon she can wade on foot but not pass under the grass. Browser emulation is not a phone.
