# stag.glb

Her mount's body and gait (STAGS.md, pass 2): the **Stag** from Quaternius's *Ultimate Animated Animal Pack*
(https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html), **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain; the pack's
License.txt: "LowPoly Models by @Quaternius … CC0 1.0 Universal … https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/").
Consider supporting the author: https://www.patreon.com/quaternius

Trimmed from the pack's `glTF/Stag.gltf` (3.2 MB, 13 clips) to a 849 KB GLB holding the mesh, the skin and five
clips, `Idle`, `Idle_Headlow`, `Walk`, `Gallop`, `Death` (the script repacked only the accessors those use; no
textures: the pack is vertex-coloured by material). In the game (`src/mountFigure.ts`) the model is dressed as her
stag of soil: every material replaced by dark loam, its horns hidden under branch antlers, grass standees on its
spine and neck, green eyes.
