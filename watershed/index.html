    HULDA'S THIRD LISTENING

    Two groves,
    one spring.

    Rain fills the spring; the spring feeds both groves; the dry days test them. Under stress the fine root joining the groves withdraws, and the way between them becomes the long deep route through the spring. When the groves drink again, it regrows.

    Watch the seasons, or hurry them. Cultivate sap with the match board. Spend 8 sap to lean the spring toward one grove (the other gets less), or 24 sap once for a moss basin that holds more of the rain through the dry.

    The same controls above and below: drag to look; thumbstick to walk or drift where you look; hold its centre for travel targets. The seasons run while you look on, and pause on the board, in this help, and when the tab is hidden.
