# The wards: ruins of the old containment

Noah, 2026-10-19, after playing the parkour. His direction is recorded first, then set against DiggyDwarves' lore and what Rootwake has already built, then the passes. R1, the surface wards, is built (end of this file). The mother herself is in MOTHER.md, the parkour in MOBILITY.md (Parkour), and the descent in DESCENT.md.

## Noah's direction

- **The traversal wants boxy, climbable ground,** and most of the world doesn't have any. That suggests ruins, as in Shadow of Mordor: clambering around them brings her into contact with lore tidbits.
- **The mountains are where magic is strongest.**
  - Chaos seeps through the crust there and crystallises.
  - Without active tending, the mountains' systems have failed.
- **The mother is tied to the magitech mountain directly.**
  - She has infested the mountain, clogging its workings and blocking the deeper levels.
  - So Hulda builds no aetherforges. She reactivates the structures that already exist.
- **Ruins ring the dark forest's periphery.**
  - Their lingering effects are why the mother has advanced no further.
  - She is strong enough to hold her terrain and send her minions to farm the villagers. She slowly gains the strength to free herself, but that is not a realised risk on the game's timescale.
- **What a ruin holds.**
  - The ruins emerge from the soil.
  - Parkour, entrenched enemies and traps guard an entrance to an instanced dungeon, like Skyrim's dwemer ruins.
  - Inside: puzzles, corrupted hollow guardians, snerk helper merchants, and greblin workers itching to get the machinery back online. Tentacle-y obstacles stymie the greblins, and they give Hulda quests to grant them access.
- **The metroidvania is not worked out.** How much does Hulda learn herself to gain access, and how much needs companions from other magitech mountains?
- **The steward.** He was brought into the village (S2), but he shouldn't be available until she has travelled a long way to his mountain.
- **The mother's form.**
  - A massive branching worm, most of its body in a huge, mostly vertical subterranean tunnel.
  - In some places it is reached from the deepened crystal waterdrop at the centre; in others only through the ruins' entrances.

## Against DiggyDwarves and Rootwake

**Agrees with DiggyDwarves almost word for word.**
- In GAME_DESIGN.md, "The karst as machine": the karst "is not a mountain. It is ancient magitech with circuitry built microscopically into the rock". It soaks up chaos, which dried "CRYSTALLIZES out of the rock". Leaks elsewhere birth abominations.
- The dwarves built it, and the free peoples were hired to maintain it. The game is "the maintenance shift, ten millennia after the last work order".
- **The greblins** run dwarf-made machinery they could never build and want working (QUEST_MANUAL.md §9b, §2B).
- **The snerks** are "the knowers", for whom commerce is their medium and not their essence. The trickster framing was dropped deliberately.
- **Doors that open as companions join.**
  - A dwarf alone "can light your own color + brute-force a couple", but "full containment needs all seven". Each recruit retro-unlocks that colour's sealed chambers in every karst (DESIGN_ADDENDUM_2026-07-09.md §5).
  - Also there: "Every descent must also be clobberable the ordinary way — the unique route is the rewarded route."
  - That is a ready answer to "learn vs. companions". Hulda learns enough to pass, and companions open the rewarded routes and the sealed colours.

**Changes Rootwake's record.**
- **MOTHER.md had Hulda building miniature aetherforges** (M3). Following Noah, the wards are the old containment's own works, and she wakes them. DiggyDwarves keeps aetherforges as the elves' far-future device, so the name is left for later. MOTHER.md now says this.
- **The older ruins (G3b, `overworldModel.ruins`)** are three leaning stone circles kept *out* of the taint. They are sanctified into groves, and EXPANSION.md §3 calls a ruin "no ongoing threat". They stay as they are, as groves.
  - The wards are a different thing: inside the taint's edge, guarded, and with an entrance.
- **The steward (S2)** sleeps at the old moot 130–260 m from the village and is woken by a channelling.
  - DiggyDwarves' steward has his own karst, the Moot, whose descent is passing the whole dungeon without killing (his verb, PARLEY).
  - Noah's note means moving his waking to his own mountain, far off, later. **Not changed yet:** it reshapes S2–S4 (duties, parties, the dire pack), so it waits for Noah to say when.
- **The mother's form.** M1 placed her as fourteen arms rising from a mound at the forest's heart, 1050 m from the karst.
  - Read with the worm: the mound is where her body breaks the surface. The body runs down and under the plateau to the karst's shaft, and in the shaft she is the "broken deep node" that dims the beam (DiggyDwarves addendum §3).
  - Her growth seals the deeper layers, so the descent's "deepening is the only way deeper" gains a second reason.
- **The hollowed.** DESCENT.md has hollowed as the stations' placid keepers. The ruins' guardians are hollowed *she has turned*: the "corrupted hollow guardians".

## Terms carried over

- **Machines:** power stations in four states (humming / browned-out / calcified / dead), junction galleries and crawl-tubes, reactor halls, breaker rooms, the beam's accumulating shaft.
- **Puzzles:** DiggyDwarves' RGB light puzzle (emitters, mirrors in 22.5° steps, targets with latches, prism and filter later); lever banks with truth tables; sprung plates; tangram gates. Chaos-crystal glass that encases a mob is a natural trap, or a way to seal a tentacle.
- **Quest grammar:**
  - the archetypes OCCUPIED, SEALED, HELD, LOST and STARVED;
  - the FIGHT / FIX / TRADE / GROW valves;
  - "Force takes objects, never knowledge";
  - "Failure transforms; it never dead-ends".
- **Lore voice:** techno-runes that light as power passes, work orders and shift logs, "limescale on the pipes".

## R1, as built (2026-10-19)

- **Placement** (`ruinsModel.wards`): `WARD_N` 5 wards on the ring `WARD_RING` 575–625 m from the breach (the taint's edge is 650).
  - Each is at the levellest walkable spot it can find, clear of every other place by `WARD_CLEAR`, and at least `WARD_APART` apart in bearing.
  - The door faces the breach, snapped to a quarter turn.
  - Each is named by its bearing from the breach ("the north-east ward").
- **The earthwork.**
  - A level court `WARD.court` 9.8 m (half-size) at the highest land under it.
  - A skirt falls smoothly to the land at no more than 1 in 3 (`SKIRT_PER`), so the ruin's faces stand on level ground and every face is exact.
  - The chunks clear its trees (`createChunks`' `clearings`).
- **The plan** (`wardPlan`; heights in `LEVEL`, all CANON):

  | Part | Size and height | Move |
  |---|---|---|
  | Apron | 7 m half, a step up | step |
  | Terrace | 5.2 m half, a mount up; one corner fallen back to the apron | mount |
  | Keep | 3.4 m half, a wall up | braced hang |
  | Crack across the keep | CANON.gap wide, down to the terrace, from 1.9 m off its centre | leap |
  | Tower | 1.2 m half, a wall more | braced hang |
  | Lintels | two on the terrace's flanks, CANON.vault high and 0.3 m deep | vault |
  | Ward-posts | four on the court beyond the apron, a wall high, or broken to a step's stump | climb, or step |

  - Broken posts are a step high, never a mount high: a post 0.9 m deep at mount height would be vaulted, and the vault's own size is the lintels'.
  - A test scans every sheer face of every turn and seed: each is a step, a mount or a wall, with nothing in between.
- **The plates.** Four per ward: on the whole post behind it, on the tower's top, on the keep's strip past the crack, and before the door. Each shows a pale pillar of light until it is read.
  - Standing on one (`READ_R`) reads it into the banner.
  - Read plates are kept in `localStorage` `rootwake-wards-read`.
- **The door.** It is in the tower's face toward the breach: a stone frame, dark within, choked with her black growth, swelling slowly. Runes in the tower's faces hum faintly.
- **In the village** (`village.ts`):
  - `wardField` answers her ground and her body's room before the dens do.
  - Stepping onto a ward's earthwork names it.
  - Dev handle: `__wards` (`list`, `field`).
- **On the practice course** (`/vista/parkour/`): one ward east of the lanes (`PRACTICE_WARD`), the same build on the flat.
- **Not yet:**
  - the wards on the map and compass;
  - the folk, wolves and Dark Young treat the ward's stone as open ground (as they do the rivers, PLATEAU.md T3);
  - the vista's grass can show through the earthwork where it stands lowest over the land.

## R2, as built (2026-10-20)

- **The ward.** The first way in is the ward nearest her village (`firstWard` in `village.ts`). Its door's growth is thinned: a greblin's chalk on the lintel says "soon".
  - Three of her blows at the door (`DOOR_CUTS`) cut the growth away. A heavy blow counts twice.
  - Every other ward's growth is "too thick to cut. Another ward first."
  - Walking into the opened door takes her inside.
- **The interior** (`wardInterior.ts`, pure; `wardInteriorWorld.ts`, drawn): an instance `INTERIOR_DEPTH` 40 m under the ward, its rooms built of the canonical heights.
  - **The vestibule.** A snerk sits there (the descent's figure) by its lantern. The way out is the door of light behind her.
  - **The gallery,** a wall's drop below (the braced hang down, a climb back). It has a fallen block to mount, the junction box (too tall to climb) and the greblin crew.
  - **The passage.** It is choked by a knot of her growth (`KNOT_CUTS` 5 blows). Until it is cut she can't pass.
  - **The crystal chamber,** with three mirrors on brass posts. One stands up on a ledge, so she must climb to it; a blow from below doesn't reach it.
    - Each blow turns a mirror 45°.
    - The crystal's light, once powered, must be turned to the ward's core on its dais, a wall up.
    - Exactly one of the 64 settings reaches the core (tested).
- **The quest** (`Quest`, saved as `rootwake-ward-r2`):
  1. `sealed`: cut the door's growth.
  2. `open`: inside, the snerk speaks; the crew asks for the knot to be cut.
  3. `cut` and `fixing`: the crew walks to the box and fixes it, badly twice (`FIX_TRIES` 3, `FIX_TRY_S` 2.6 s each). The lamp flares, then goes green.
  4. `powered`: the crystal lights and throws its beam.
  5. `woken`: the mirrors bring the light to the core.
  - The mirrors can be set before the power comes; the ward then wakes on the crew's last try.
- **A woken ward.**
  - Its runes burn bright, a pillar of pale light rises from its tower, and its door stands open.
  - Within `WARD_CALM_R` 170 m the Colour falls back. The taint's depth, as the sky, fog, breath and flares read it, is lowered by up to `WARD_CALM` 0.7 at the ward.
  - The land's baked grey is unchanged.
- **The words** (`SNERK_LINES`, `CREW_LINES`, `WAKE_LINE`, `DOOR_LINES`) are placeholder for Noah. The snerk trades nothing yet ("when you have anything worth trading").
- **Dev handle:** `__wards`: `first`, `quest`, `inside`, `interior()`, `toDoor()`, `resetQuest()`.
- **Not yet:**
  - the snerk's trade, and what it knows of the other doors;
  - the crew or the woken ward doing anything for her afterward;
  - foes and traps (R3).

## The industrial ruins, I1 (Noah, 2026-10-21: "add many more")

CONTAGION.md's brief: "one of the great things about the Pine Barrens is the ruined remains of former industry … some with clear lessons to teach, goals, quests, enemies to kill, power to unlock; some inscrutable and lost to time, present to be clambered over and wondered about". I1 is the second half: places to clamber and wonder at, each with a stone or two of lore. The lessons, goals and power are I2.

- **The model** (`industryModel.ts`, pure). Each kind has a plan, built like the wards only of the canonical heights on a level earthwork. Mounds of slag, cullet or spoil lie wholly on the yard, never against a face.
  - A test scans every face of every kind in every orientation: each is a step, a mount or a wall, or too high to climb (≥ 1.75 m).
  - Another test drives her by the stick from the furnace's casting floor up its bank and onto its stack.

  | Kind | Yard | Ground it wants |
  |---|---|---|
  | Bog-iron furnace (2) | The casting floor a step up, the bank a mount then a wall, the stack a wall above the bank and too high from the floor. The tuyere arch in the stack's face, the casting house's broken walls, a slag heap. | Dry |
  | Glassworks | The melting house's walls (wall-high and mount-high lengths with gaps between), the hearth, the leer to walk along, cullet heaps glinting with glass. | Dry |
  | Charcoal pits (2) | Earth rings a step high round blackened burns; the collier's hut, its walls a mount high; a log pile. | Dry |
  | Cranberry bog | Three long dikes a mount high and their cross-dikes, the red beds between, the sluice a leap wide between posts too high to climb. | A river's bank, along it |
  | Paper mill | Walls a wall high with doorways; the race, two walls a mount high a leap apart; the wheel at its head, a gear in the grass. | A river's bank |
  | Sand pit | The spoil banked in white sand, each terrace one move up and narrower, a rusted cart on the third. | Dry |
  | Railbed | The embankment 60 m long, its ties and broken rails, the platform a mount high, the water tower's legs (too high) and its tank. | Dry, the land rising little |
  | Drowned town | Foundations a step high, chimneys a wall high or whole, the chapel's steps and floor and its tower stump. The rest of the street lies under the lake past the yard's edge. | The lake's edge |
  | Corduroy road | Cedar logs laid crosswise on a raised causeway; stumps beside it. | A river's bank |

- **Placement** (`industries`, `industryLand`): each kind's count, by the seed, on a lattice over the plateau.
  - Out in the wilds (past the villages' meadows) and on walkable ground.
  - `INDUSTRY_CLEAR` 60 m clear of every place (her taint among them, as the lair's radius) and of the wards, and apart from each other.
  - Where the land rises least under the yard (`WANT[kind].rise`).
  - Named after the Barrens' ghost towns ("the Harrisville furnace", "drowned Speedwell").
  - Seed 1 places eleven.
- **The drawing** (`industryWorld.ts`): ironstone, brick, fieldstone, sand, peat and timber, each with its own texture; the earthwork coloured by kind, its mounds by what they are; the decor; stones of lore that glow amber, with a pillar of light until read (the wards' plates are pale blue).
- **In the village** (`village.ts`):
  - The yards' trees are cleared (`createChunks`' clearings) and so is the understory (`LookInputs.clearings`).
  - Her ground and her body's room come from the field, after the wards'.
  - A yard is named as she steps onto it, and a stone is read where she stands on it, kept in `rootwake-yards-read`.
  - On the map and the compass, a yard shows as an amber chimney-and-square once she has explored its ground.
  - Dev handle: `__industry` (`list`, `field`).
- **Lore:** `INDUSTRY_LORE`, placeholder for Noah. It ties the iron, the lenses, the paper's unfading ink and the drowned town to the world's own story.
- **I2, proposed:**
  - situations from the quest grammar: the sealed furnace (its arch opens on a hearth that still burns), the held mill (something nests in the wheel pit), the lost town (a dive under the lake);
  - the Leeds Devil as the Barrens' own creature;
  - a few yards as places of power for the contagion's front (irritants or germination points, with the land session).
