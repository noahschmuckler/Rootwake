    HULDA'S FOURTH LISTENING

    A pillar of stone,
    and the roots that hold it.

    You wake on the narrow top of a karst. The plants here cling to bare limestone; their roots drape over its faces and thread its body. Root vision shows the stone glassy. Commune with a plant and you shrink into its root and ride it like water to the next plant on the chain. One root arrives inside the mountain.

    At the foot, the forest has soil: sink into it and drift where you look. Cultivate sap with the match board there. A spring at the foot feeds two groves through the seasons; under stress the fine roots withdraw, and with them the quick ways. Sap can lean the spring, hold more rain, or wake the pine's dormant taproot.

    Drag to look, on the ground, in the soil and while riding. Thumbstick to walk or drift; hold its centre for targets. Find the forest at the foot, then a way back to the top.
