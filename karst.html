HULDA'S FOURTH LISTENING

A pillar of stone,
and the roots that hold it.

You wake on the narrow top of a karst. There is no soil to sink into: the plants here cling to bare limestone, and their roots drape over its faces and thread through its body.

Root vision shows the stone glassy and the roots bright. Commune with a plant and you shrink into its root and ride it like water to the next plant on the chain. There, emerge, or ride on. One root arrives inside the mountain.

Drag to look, on the ground and while riding. Thumbstick to walk; hold its centre for targets. Find your way to the forest at the foot, then back up to the top by another way.

Where you are and which plants you have met save here. Reload resumes at the last plant. Earlier studies' saves are separate.
