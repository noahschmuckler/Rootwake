# The arc: DELIVER, REVILED, the mother, and the next karst

Design discussion with Noah (2026-10-29), a draft spec, not a pass. Nothing here is built. It ties the catwalk letters
(`OPENING_ARC.md`, `DESCENT.md`) to the ecology (`ECOLOGY.md`) and the six heroes (`COMPANIONS.md`, `ADVERSARIES.md`). Lines marked
**Noah** are his decisions; **Claude** lines are readings or proposals; **Open** lines wait on him.

## Seven letters, seven stages (Noah)

DELIVER has seven letters. **D is observation.** The other six (**E, L, I, V, E, R**) each end with the awakening of one archetypal
expert among the hobbits. Each awakening adds to the village's population and abilities and **raises the danger to the villagers.**

### D: observation

1. **She wakes** atop the karst and sees creatures only as **icons or outlines,** magnified through **water-droplet telescopes,**
   with no understanding or insight.
2. **The snerks send her out as a deer** to observe and gather data (`ECOLOGY.md`). Spirit deer are soil and branches, so she can walk
   the world unable to interact with it.
3. **Watching reactivates the instruments** on the catwalk. Each behaviour she observes gives the instruments something to explain,
   so she comes to understand the creatures and the ecology.
4. **She learns the ecology as it first is, across the seasons,** before anything is disturbed.

### The turn: the hobbits' wish

- She realizes the hobbits wish to have children. She goes to them **as other animals, on fetch quests,** each sent by a people:

| She comes as | Brings | Sent by |
|---|---|---|
| a **deer** | chicken-of-the-woods | the snerks |
| a **bobcat** | fish | the Hollowed |
| a **boar** | acorns | the greeblins |

- (Claude: this is the Eden's break from the hobbits' side. The gifts are the diet that makes children possible, and the animals' kin
  peoples are the ones who send her. It sets the overharvest in motion only after she has watched the world through several sets of eyes.)

### The revelations (E, L, I, V, E, R)

- **The early abilities that the six civilizational heroes will later develop first emerge spontaneously among the hobbits.** As the
  hobbits' **diet improves, they have revelations.**
- **Magic mushrooms** may be worth building in: they grow **only under special circumstances.** Hulda, as a deer, **lies down in the
  soil among the hobbits and spends her accumulated cultivated energy to become a patch of magic mushrooms.** Eating them gives the
  hobbits **visions.**
- From the visions, one hobbit at a time becomes an archetype:

| Hobbit becomes | Does | Counterpart (`ADVERSARIES.md`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Steward** | organizes hunting parties | Moloch |
| **Mason** | builds the first huts | Yog-Sothoth |
| **Sage** | writes knowledge down so it is not lost when the thunderbird takes the elder who became a mason, steward or other | the King in Yellow |
| **Smith** | prepares copper tools | the Chthonians |
| **Alchemist** | invents chemical fertilizers and poisons | Ubbo-Sathla |
| **Artificer** | crafts simple machines to enhance lives and capabilities | the Mi-Go (three in a trenchcoat) |

- Noah listed them in this order; **which letter each takes is not stated** (Open).
- (Claude: the sage's job is the elder-culling made practical. The thunderbird takes the elder who held the knowledge, and the sage's
  writing is the answer. It is also why the first record the hobbits keep is a defence against the failsafes.)

## REVILED (Noah)

- After DELIVER, the next deepening **spells REVILED** and unlocks a **second tier of abilities** for the six masters and for Hulda.
  The tier lets them **resist and defeat the thunderbird, the bear, the hostile wolves and the bobcats.**
- **At the end of REVILED the villages are established in their places,** farming the world and **growing quickly.**
- (Claude: this is where the letters' meaning turns. DELIVER reads as service down the mountain; REVILED reads as the price of it.
  It also follows the earlier ruling that the top catwalk is D, going down spells DELIVER and going up spells REVILED
  (`OPENING_ARC.md`). Noah's message says "the next deepening spells out reviled," which may be a different structure from
  ascending. Open.)

## The mother: from failsafe to fail-state (Noah)

- Quick growth **awakens the mother,** a step beyond the failsafes into a **fail-state:** she **may genuinely devour the entire plateau
  and all its inhabitants,** unless Hulda defeats her **from land, air, underwater and underground** (`MOTHER.md`: the breach, the
  shaft, the branching worm).
- **Defeating her stabilizes the plateau, but genetic variability becomes a real problem.**

## Expansion and the next karst (Noah)

- **The solution is expansion:** lead the villagers **down to comingle with the primitive inhabitants of the world below,** bringing
  agriculture and **the basic tech tree of the elder hobbits.**
- **Further advancement needs a journey to another karst-plateau.** A similar path plays out there, with an **enhanced tech tree
  unlocked by the civilizational hero present** and a **unique end-boss counterpoint.**
- (Claude: this is `VISION.md`'s eight corners seen from the first. The first plateau is the template; each later one repeats it with
  more.)

## How this lands on what is built (Claude)

- The **pool's sight, deepening and cultivation tiers** (G4a, G5) are the D stage's frame; the telescopes are a new look at an existing
  HUD idea.
- The **steward, hunting parties, larder, searching parties** (`villageModel.ts`, `SETTLEMENTS.md`) already exist and would become the
  steward's awakening instead of a given.
- The **Descent's keepers** already include a snerk archive on every layer, the Hollowed from layer 2 and the greeblins from layer 4.
  That matches the three peoples who send her as a deer, bobcat and boar.
- The **mother's brood mechanics** (`MOTHER.md`, the D3 block) are the fail-state's fighting side.

## Open

- **Letter order.** Which of E, L, I, V, E, R is each master? (A letter pair repeats E twice, so two masters share a letter.)
- **Danger per master.** What new danger does each awakening add to the villagers, and does it feed its counterpart in
  `ADVERSARIES.md`?
- **Magic mushroom cost.** How much cultivated energy, and does each patch grow only once?
- **Observation's pace.** How many seasons must she watch before the instruments let her act? Is that a fixed clock or her choice?
- **The turn.** Does she realize the hobbits' wish by observation alone, or does Petal show her?
- **REVILED structure.** Is it ascent back up the same catwalks, or a second descent?
- **The mother's four fronts.** Land, air, underwater, underground: are they four phases, or four places she must be at once?
- **Genetic variability.** Is it a number the player sees, or a story event?
