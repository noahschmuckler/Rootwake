# The arc: DELIVER, REVILED, the mother, and the next karst

Design discussion with Noah (2026-10-29), a draft spec, not a pass. Nothing here is built. It ties the catwalk letters
(`OPENING_ARC.md`, `DESCENT.md`) to the ecology (`ECOLOGY.md`) and the six heroes (`COMPANIONS.md`, `ADVERSARIES.md`). Lines marked
**Noah** are his decisions; **Claude** lines are readings or proposals; **Open** lines wait on him.

## The rule of every layer (Noah)

- **Each layer has a stability of its own,** so she is never forced to hurry to the next.
- **She can hold back a layer's challenges by hand.** Completing the layer's quests **solves** a problem: it makes something automatic,
  defeats a threat, or reroutes one. That **unlocks the next challenge arc.**
- **Seasons.** The first (observation) phase **spans all four seasons.** Each later layer probably does too, **or can be completed only
  in a certain season** (winter when the bear sleeps, spring when the blueberries are plentiful, and so on).
- **Cultivation is a standard resource that is not spent,** like the villagers' accumulated prayer. It grows as she meditates and
  cultivates. **At a threshold of it, plus certain quest objectives, she can appear as the spirit deer and sprout mushrooms.**
  (This replaces the earlier idea that she spends it to become the mushrooms.)

## DELIVER: seven letters, seven stages (Noah)

**D is observation.** The six letters **E, L, I, V, E, R** each end with the awakening of one archetypal hobbit expert. Each adds
population and abilities and **raises the danger to the villagers.**

### D: observation

1. **She wakes** atop the karst and sees creatures only as **icons or outlines,** magnified through **water-droplet telescopes,** with
   no understanding.
2. **The snerks send her out as a deer** to observe and gather data (`ECOLOGY.md`). A spirit deer is soil and branches, so she can walk
   the world unable to interact with it.
3. **Watching reactivates the instruments** on the catwalk. Each behaviour she observes lets them explain more, so she comes to
   understand the creatures and the ecology.
4. **She learns the ecology as it first is, across all four seasons,** before anything is disturbed.

### The turn: the hobbits' wish

She realizes the hobbits wish to have children. She goes to them **as other animals, on fetch quests,** each sent by a people:

| She comes as | Brings | Sent by |
|---|---|---|
| a **deer** | chicken-of-the-woods | the snerks |
| a **bobcat** | fish | the Hollowed |
| a **boar** | acorns | the greeblins |

### The revelations

- **The early abilities the six heroes will later develop first emerge spontaneously among the hobbits.** As their **diet improves,
  they have revelations.**
- **Magic mushrooms** grow **only under special circumstances:** at the cultivation threshold and with quest objectives done, Hulda as
  a spirit deer lies down among the hobbits and **sprouts a patch.** Eating it gives the hobbits **visions.**
- One hobbit at a time becomes an archetype.

### Letters, masters and the danger each brings

**Claude's proposal, for Noah to accept or change** (Noah left the order to me). The order is the one Noah listed, and the sage is
third because she writes after the thunderbird has taken an elder (steward or mason), so two must exist first.

| Letter | Master | Hobbits gain | Danger (from `ADVERSARIES.md`, shown in this knowledge) | Layer quest (solves a threat, unlocks the next) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E** | **Steward** | hunting parties, shared stores | **Moloch**: competition without coordination. Parties race one another for the same patch and the same chicken-of-the-woods; defection pays. | Set a tally or rotation all parties keep, so the harvest is automatic and fair. |
| **L** | **Mason** | the first huts | **Yog-Sothoth and the Hounds of Tindalos**: every door is also a gate. Huts bring doors, and doors open where they should not. | Seal a breach by rounding the corners and closing the gate (curves seal, angles open). |
| **I** | **Sage** | writing, so knowledge outlives the elders | **The King in Yellow**: truth without mercy. A text that is true and leaves the reader without hope. | Add a gloss or a reader's circle to the text, so the writing carries context as well as fact. |
| **V** | **Smith** | copper tools | **The Chthonians**: digging without restraint. Mining brings heat, quakes and hollowed ground. | Shore the galleries and set a limit to the digging. |
| **E** | **Alchemist** | fertilizers and poisons | **Ubbo-Sathla**: dissolution without form. Runoff (Claude: would foul the river eddies where the fish-fungus is born). | Build filters and quarantine, so the water stays clean. |
| **R** | **Artificer** | simple machines | **The Mi-Go, as the Showman**: spectacle without wisdom. Machines and light that mesmerize. | Tie the machines to the sage's context, so power comes with wisdom. |

- The pattern in the quests is the same as in `ADVERSARIES.md`'s engine table: each adversary wins through its network, and each
  hero's quest installs the opposite (kept treaties, curves, glosses, shoring, filters, wisdom).
- (Claude: the thunderbird's culling is the clock that makes the sage necessary, and the alchemist's runoff is the link back to the
  fish cycle, which makes the ecology the place where the dangers show.)

## REVILED: the continued descent (Noah)

- **DELIVER is where knowledge is delivered. REVILED is the deepening beyond it,** and what leads to **killing the thunderbird and the
  bear and awakening the mother.**
- It is a **continuing descent,** mirroring the **kabbalistic Tree of Life and, in its inverse, the broken shells of the Tree of
  Death** (the Qliphoth).
- It unlocks a **second tier of abilities** for the six masters and for Hulda, so they can **resist and defeat the thunderbird, the
  bear, the hostile wolves and the bobcats.** At its end **the villages are established in their places,** farming the world and
  growing quickly.
- **Claude's observation, for Noah to check.** DELIVER ends in R and REVILED begins with R. If the column continues down from the R
  catwalk, sharing it, the whole column reads **DELIVEREVILED**, thirteen letters, the same upward and downward, with R as the nadir.
  Going down it reads DELIVER, then EVILED. It also fits the earlier ruling that going up reads REVILED. Whether the shared R is
  intended, and whether the column has seven or thirteen catwalks, is Open. (`LETTERS` in `src/descentModel.ts` holds seven.)

## The mother: from failsafe to fail-state (Noah)

Quick growth **awakens the mother,** a step beyond the failsafes: she **may genuinely devour the plateau and everyone on it** unless
Hulda defeats her **by land, air, water and underground.** The four fronts are **separate metroidvania pathways** (`MOTHER.md`: the
breach, the shaft, the branching worm).

| Front | Hulda | Gains |
|---|---|---|
| **Land** | activates **wards** to limit the spread, fights **Dark Young,** protects the villages | |
| **Air** | attacks the mother from above, **dropping bombs or parachuting onto her tentacles** | **flight,** possibly after killing the thunderbird |
| **Water** | dives deep into the lake to **untangle the tentacles from the karst's workings** | **swimming** |
| **Underground** | delves ruins, activates **more powerful wards,** fights **Dark Young variants,** **corrupted Hollowed** as she commandeers power stations, **corrupted greeblins** as she reclaims machinery and forges, plus **unique monstrosities, cavernous mazes and puzzles** | |

**After she defeats the mother the plateau stabilizes.** Then **genetic variability** becomes the reason to leave. It is **stated, not
shown as unwanted mutations:** the problem is **stagnation,** with not much new to do or accomplish.

## Expansion and the next karst (Noah)

- **Expansion:** lead the villagers **down to comingle with the primitive inhabitants of the world below,** bringing agriculture and
  **the elder hobbits' basic tech tree.**
- **Further advancement needs another karst-plateau,** where a similar path plays out with an **enhanced tech tree unlocked by the
  civilizational hero there** and a **unique end-boss counterpoint.**

## How this lands on what is built (Claude)

- The pool's sight, deepening and cultivation tiers (G4a, G5) are the D stage's frame; the telescopes are a new look at an existing HUD.
- The steward, hunting parties, larder and search parties (`villageModel.ts`, `SETTLEMENTS.md`) already exist and become the steward's
  awakening instead of a given.
- The Descent's keepers (a snerk archive on every layer, Hollowed from layer 2, greeblins from layer 4) match the three peoples who send
  her as a deer, bobcat and boar.
- The mother's brood mechanics (`MOTHER.md`, the D3 block) are the fail-state's land front.
