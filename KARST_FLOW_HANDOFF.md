## The second pass: every tree, a dense forest, the sisters climbed
Noah asked: from now on every tree that renders acts the same (enter the trunk and rise to the top, or sink to the roots and travel by root), denser trees below the karst, and pathways to the top of neighbouring karsts by leaf-hopping and by root. Built:
- **Every tree is a node** (`NODES` in `karstFlowModel.ts`): the karst's own plants, a generated forest of some 420 trees at the foot (dense within 40 m of the karst and along corridors to the two climbable sisters, sparser beyond, out to 92 m), a foot tree and a helix of clinging ledge trees up each climbable sister, and their summit pines. A node has its trunk radius, crown height and radius, a lean (the face trees) and a root mouth. The fern is the one plant with no trunk: pressing into it goes straight to its roots.
- **The clearing's moves on all of them.** Press into a trunk: she goes in as the bark bulge (`trunk` mode) and the stick climbs it; at the top she is the leaf figure at the crown's edge (`crown`), sideways slides her round, pushing toward a crown within `HOP_REACH` (9 m sideways, `HOP_RISE` 8 m up or down) leaps her across (`hop`); down goes back into the trunk; at the trunk's foot, pushing down sinks her to the root mouth, where the stick chooses a root as before. A double tap of the stick brings her out of a trunk, a crown or a mouth onto the ground beside the tree (`standNear`: the first clear spot round its trunk). Rising into a crown, the stick must be let go once before a push leaps her, so the climb's push does not become a leap toward whatever crown lies that way; a crown reached by a leap is armed at once, so a held stick chains leaps.
- **The root network.** Every floor tree is joined to its nearest three within 9.5 m by roots that sag under the soil, and the pieces are joined by their closest pairs until the network is one; the soil goes glassy while she rides it. All roots (the karst's nine, the sisters' four, the network's 680) are one mesh with a colour per vertex; the chosen or ridden root is painted bright. Motes run only on the long roots.
- **The sisters.** The pillar at (60, -30), 78 m, and the one at (-55, 40), 58 m, are limestone now, with tufts, moss, a summit disc and pine, and a helix of twelve (B) or nine (C) shelf ledges each with a leaning tree, spaced for the leap (`HELIX`). Leaf route: floor trees to the sister's foot tree, up the helix crown to crown, from the last ledge onto the summit pine. Root route: from the foot tree up the face to the mid-helix tree, then over the rim to the pine (two capillary climbs of about 45 m each, so about 15 s each; downhill they run). The other four pillars stay in the mist. Tested: both summits reachable by root and by leaf from the karst's foot, and back.
- **Progress** records each sister summit reached (`summits`); the save key is now `rootwake-karst-flow-v2`.
- **Camera** knows every pillar's rock (`insideRock` over `PILLARS`).

## The brief
After the clearing's free flow and the character's gait fix, Noah asked for the same movement methodology applied to the karst: exclude the springs, keep the bespoke karst and its unique root and vine connections, all in third person, no tooltips on the screen, so as to see whether the natural movement works without buttons to choose destinations.

## What is built
- **The karst, as it was.** The pillar, its silhouette, the cavern with its pool and mushroom light, the three ledges, the summit pine, the shrubs and the fig clinging to the faces, the fern, the oak and the maple at the foot, the wild forest, the tufts and moss on the faces, the sister pillars in mist, the opaque bark roots with their collars: all from `karstModel.ts` and the karst world, rebuilt in `karstFlowWorld.ts` without the spring, the basin, the groves' ledger, the rain, the soil volume or the board. Nine of the ten roots are ridden: the dormant taproot belonged to the sap economy and is left out; the foot root (oak to maple round the south of the foot) is always open.
- **Hulda on the X Bot's skeleton.** Since the character branch was merged in, she is the rigged Hulda (`CHARACTER_HANDOFF.md`): her own leaf-and-bark body on the X Bot's 65 bones, driven by the Mixamo idle / walking / running clips in `public/models/clips`, loaded at start and falling back to her procedural gait on the same bones if they fail. `?body=xbot` shows the X Bot body with the same clips. The deploy copies `dist/models` beside the page.
- **Third person, Hulda, one stick.** The shared player in its third view with the rigged character; the same drag orbits the camera in every form (since the village's V0.2 every form's camera is the shared third person's rule: behind her by yaw, a fixed lift, the look tilted by pitch, so a drag reads the same whatever she is). The camera pulls in to her eye rather than enter the limestone (`insideRock`). One small button switches to first person and back; the other is help. No hint line, no labels, no bearing, no story text; the intro dialog is three sentences.
- **Zones.** Each plant's ground is its own small world for the shared player (`makeZoneWorld`): the summit disc, the ledges (with the pillar's face as a wall), the cavern floor round its pool, the forest floor round the pillar's foot. Edges hold her; there is no falling.
- **Into a plant.** Walking into a plant pauses her; pressing on (the stick held toward it for 0.35 s) takes her into its roots: she sinks as a knot of wood to the root mouth at its foot (0.7 s), and the limestone goes glassy while she is in the network. A double tap of the stick within 3.2 m of a plant does the same (since the village's V0.2 the double tap lives on the stick only; the screen has none).
- **The stick chooses the root.** At a mouth, after a short beat, pushing the stick the way a root visibly sets off (its departure three metres in, projected to the screen from where the camera is) picks it; the chosen root brightens gold while the stick points at it, and held for 0.3 s it is taken. Roots behind the camera cannot be chosen; the root she just arrived by is not offered until she lets go of the stick once, so a held stick does not bounce her back.
- **The ride is the karst's.** Water-slide kinematics unchanged (`stepRide`: downhill pulls to 13 m/s, uphill is a 3 m/s capillary climb, braking over the last 9 m). She rides as the knot along the root's centreline; the camera follows the root's own heading gently and a drag still offsets it. At the far plant she is at its mouth again: the stick chooses on, or a double tap of the stick grows her back onto that plant's ground (0.8 s).
- **Her trail.** Every cell she walks deepens with every pass and flowering tufts grow there, per zone (the summit and the cavern share x,z). Trail, plant met, the first arrival on the floor and the return to the summit save locally (`rootwake-karst-flow-v1`) and on page hide; reload wakes her on the plant she was last in.

## Tuning to judge on the phone
- Does the stick's choice of root read without text: is the brightening enough, and is the 0.3 s hold right? Where two roots set off the same way on screen (at the east shrub, the cavern root and the south root both go down), does orbiting the camera make the choice clear, or does the mouth need to turn her to face the chosen root?
- The 0.45 s beat after arriving: long enough that a held stick does not skip a plant, short enough not to feel like a stop?
- Whether a double tap for coming out is discoverable with only the intro to say so, or whether pushing the stick where no root goes should grow her out.
- The camera pulling in on ledges when she faces away from the pillar; the ride camera through the glassy stone.
- The knot as her in the roots; whether the slide wants the knot smaller or the camera closer.
- Whether the taproot should return as an always-open fast way up, and whether the trail belongs on a pillar with no soil.

## Honest limits
An authored pillar and graph, not procedural karst. The ride camera has no collision with the rock: the stone is glassy while she rides. No falling from ledges. Browser emulation is not a phone.
