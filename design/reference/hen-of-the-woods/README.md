## What the images agree on (Noah's likes)

- **Eyes on thin curved stalks** rising from the top tier.
- **Short, thick, pale mushroom-stem legs,** a waddle.
- **Shelf tiers that are half-moons** with a ruffled, banded front edge (orange, cream, rust, grey) and a straight back edge.
- **A flat, rough, bark-textured back** (a torn slab), with a pale scar where it came off the tree.
- Mycelium threads and splinters when it tears free.

## What the life cycle needs from the model (see `ECOLOGY.md`)

A **lean** young hen (few tiers, long legs), middle stages, and a **fat** one (broad body, short legs, a meaty pale centre); a **stunned**
pose (rolled onto its back, righting itself); a **bud** on a tree base (pale lumpy cluster) and the **sealed patch** it grows from.
Fatness should read from the silhouette alone.

## Two forms (Noah's idea, 2026-11-01)

The walking body is a **round, fluffed rosette.** To attach, it **swivels its tiers to one side** and exposes a flat, bark-textured attachment
face against the trunk; after tearing free it **fluffs back out** to the round form. A sphere also rolls, which suits the stun-and-roll-upright.
