## The dress images (2026-11-01, Noah's verdicts)

| File | Verdict |
|---|---|
| `dress-walk-hoist-sit.jpg` | **Close enough to start the 3D model.** Walking fluffed up, approaching with the gown lifted (bare white bottom), backing toward the tree, sitting with the dress bunched round. Ignore the smiling face on the first panel. |
| `dress-harvest-flat-dress.jpg` | Panel 1 is **too gory** (a wolf pulling out a bloody mass). **Panels 2 and 3 work:** the flat splayed dress, then two eyestalks popping up from its middle. |
| `dress-escape-miniskirt.jpg` | **Right direction:** a lanky-legged little creature springing out of the flat dress in a miniskirt of a few brackets. **Drop the arms and the face** (eyestalks with eyes only). |
| `dress-relief-sit.jpg` | Hits the emotional beat ("whew") but **not the action wanted**: it should run up, flip the skirt briefly, and sit with legs tucked. |
| `dress-regrowth-five.jpg` | **Right direction:** the skirt grows from a few brackets to a full gown. **Legs must be tucked in against the tree, not splayed forward; no arms, no face.** |

**How it is hunted (Noah):** flip it upside down; the dress falls; the bare bottom and legs kick in the air. A wolf or hobbit steps on the dress
and pulls the legs until the piece tears free: **a mushroom stem with a small colourful cap, a bare cartoon bottom and two floppy fat
legs.** (Keep it comic, not bloody.)

**What comes out and what regrows (Noah):** the piece is the legs, bottom and part of the torso, topped with a soft mushroom cap that was *inserted into the chest.* The eyestalks retract in. Flat on the ground, the creature grows folded lanky legs, drawing energy from the largest lower brackets and keeping only the top few young ones; then it springs out in a miniskirt and its eyestalks resprout.

## What the images agree on (Noah's likes)

- **Eyes on thin curved stalks** rising from the top tier.
- **Short, thick, pale mushroom-stem legs,** a waddle.
- **Shelf tiers that are half-moons** with a ruffled, banded front edge (orange, cream, rust, grey) and a straight back edge.
- **A flat, rough, bark-textured back** (a torn slab), with a pale scar where it came off the tree.
- Mycelium threads and splinters when it tears free.

## What the life cycle needs from the model (see `ECOLOGY.md`)

A **lean** young hen (few tiers, long legs), middle stages, and a **fat** one (broad body, short legs, a meaty pale centre); a **stunned**
pose (rolled onto its back, righting itself); a **bud** on a tree base (pale lumpy cluster) and the **sealed patch** it grows from.
Fatness should read from the silhouette alone.

## The dress (Noah, 2026-11-01)

The tiers are **a gown of tresses** worn by a small pale creature. It walks **fluffed up,** then at a tree **hoists the gown** (a bare white bottom),
**backs onto the trunk** and sits with the dress **ruffled up behind it round its neck.** When harvested it is left as a **flat, splayed dress**,
then **springs out on stilt legs in a miniskirt** of a few small brackets and runs to a budded tree to sit and regrow. Reference for the sitting
gown: `backing-onto-tree-best.jpg`; for the lean stilt-legged stage: `life-stages-four.jpg` (stage 1).

**Note on generation:** editing an image with Gemini kept putting the bark face on the wrong side (front against the tree). A fresh prompt that says the creature *backs against* the trunk, with no log named, worked. Prefer fresh generation.
