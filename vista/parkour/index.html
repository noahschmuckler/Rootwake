ROOTWAKE · PARKOUR

The stick alone

Push the stick the way she should go. A step she steps; a waist-high top she mounts; a wall she jumps to and hangs from, and pauses: push toward the wall to climb on, away to drop. A thin rail at a run she vaults; off a high ledge she lowers herself into the hang, and pauses the same way; a gap she is running at she leaps.

Every obstacle here is one of the canonical heights, each the size of its animation. Left lane: step, mount, wall, and a ledge at its far end. Middle: rails, a mount's block, a wall to climb and drop from, a gap. Right: a wall too high and a pit too wide. Far right: the old bridge's foot. East of it, a ward: a ruin of the old containment, as they stand about the dark forest. Climb to the glowing plates to read them.
