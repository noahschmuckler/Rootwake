ROOTWAKE · PARKOUR

The stick alone

Push the stick the way she should go. Where the ground rises past a step she steps, clambers, mantles or climbs; a thin rail at a run she vaults; off an edge she hops, or turns and lowers herself into a hang and drops; a gap she is running at she leaps. Nothing else to press.

Left lane: a stair of rises, one move each, and a sheer drop at its far end. Middle: rails, a block, a pit. Right: a wall too high and a pit too wide. Far right: the old bridge's steep foot.
