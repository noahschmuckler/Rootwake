ROOTWAKE · VILLAGE 1

The Village

Eight hobbits in six houses. They go out at dawn to gather what the land gives, carry it to the stores on the green, eat from them three times a day, and are home by dark. Stand in a ring at a place to gather for them, in a ring at a store to hand it over; when the stores hold enough, they go to the stone and pray. Stand before the stone to spend their prayer. After dark the Dark Young come out of the wood for the stores: outgather them, or fight. Pinch to see from above; pinch on past that for the map of what she has seen. Somewhere out there a dark forest drains whoever walks in it; at its heart the mother of goats waits, and each Dark Young slain makes her a little more. Their day is twenty minutes of yours and passes only while you are here. They do not see you yet. Double tap the stick to go under the grass or into a root, and again to come out.
Ask a miracle of the stone
The world as she has seen it. Drag to look about; spread to return.
