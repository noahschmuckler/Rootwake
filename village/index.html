ROOTWAKE · VILLAGE 1

The Village

A village of hobbits in six houses. They go out at dawn to gather what the land gives, carry it to the stores on the green, eat from them three times a day, and are home by dark. Fed through every meal for days, a child is born; going without, they weaken, then die, and the village mourns. Stand in a ring at a place to gather for them, in a ring at a store to hand it over; when the stores hold enough, they go to the stone and pray. Their prayer is yours to spend from anywhere (the pray button): a spirit to do a job, a quickening that fills a place at once, or a new hut. The fight button opens the strike, the thorn burst and the root bind. More of them than beds and the village sets the stakes for a hut itself; the freed gatherers fetch wood and water to them and build, and your stack of wood or water goes straight in. After dark the Dark Young come out of the wood for the stores, then devour a place of the land and leave their own food behind; a spoiled place gives nothing for a day, and when nothing else is left the villagers eat the leavings, and breed on them. A beaten Dark Young does not die: it runs flat and fast for its mother. Root it and stand by it while her sap lasts, and it withers in the roots and melts: one at a time. And at night something small comes out of the dark forest for the village's infants. Strike it and it drops what it carries; carry the infant home to its door. What it takes the mother keeps for a day, where she can be delved for it; then it becomes a Dark Young, for no Dark Young is without an infant inside it. Melt that one, and the infant lies where it was. Pinch to see from above; pinch on past that for the map of what she has seen. Other villages lie out on the land, the same folk under other names, with wolves of their own; what the villagers say of them and of the dens goes to the map as a hint, and whichever village you stand in is the one your rings, your prayer and your fight belong to. Somewhere out there a dark forest drains whoever walks in it; at its heart the mother of goats waits, and each Dark Young slain makes her a little more. Their day is twenty minutes of yours and passes only while you are here. They do not see you yet. Double tap the stick to go under the grass, and again to come out. Under the grass, one tap takes the nearest root and one tap leaves it. On the map, tap a place and a way is plotted through the roots: the tree to enter glows; press into it and the roots carry you, one tap to get off. Press into a tree at the foot of the karst and its roots offer their places. On the karst's summit a stream falls from the sky into a crystal pool: stand in it and meditate (the board gathers clarity), then dive: down the taproot to the deep node, where the world hangs above you and the deep roots reach every shrine and root convergence within the node's reach; pick one and you are launched to it. A village is shrined to you once enough prayer has been given at its stone; from a shrine or a convergence, meditate again to return to the deep. Shrine enough villages and the node can be deepened at the pool, widening its reach.
