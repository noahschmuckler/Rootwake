ROOTWAKE · VILLAGE 1

The Village

A village of hobbits in six houses. They go out at dawn to gather what the land gives, carry it to the stores on the green, eat from them three times a day, and are home by dark. Fed through every meal for days, a child is born; going without, they weaken, then die, and the village mourns. Stand in a ring at a place to gather for them, in a ring at a store to hand it over; when the stores hold enough, they go to the stone and pray. Stand before the stone to spend their prayer. After dark the Dark Young come out of the wood for the stores, then devour a place of the land and leave their own food behind; a spoiled place gives nothing for a day, and when nothing else is left the villagers eat the leavings, and breed on them. A beaten Dark Young does not die: it runs flat and fast for its mother. Root it and stand by it, and it withers in the roots and melts. Pinch to see from above; pinch on past that for the map of what she has seen. Somewhere out there a dark forest drains whoever walks in it; at its heart the mother of goats waits, and each Dark Young slain makes her a little more. Their day is twenty minutes of yours and passes only while you are here. They do not see you yet. Double tap the stick to go under the grass, and again to come out. Under the grass, one tap takes the nearest root and one tap leaves it. On the map, tap a place and a way is plotted through the roots: the tree to enter glows; press into it and the roots carry you, one tap to get off. Press into a tree at the foot of the karst and its roots offer their places.
Ask a miracle of the stone
The world as she has seen it. Drag to look about; spread to return. Tap a place to plot a way through the roots; tap the mark again to forget it.
