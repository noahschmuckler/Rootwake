ROOTWAKE · VILLAGE 1

The Village

Eight hobbits in six houses. They go out at dawn to gather what the land gives, carry it to the stores on the green, eat from them at noon and at dusk, and are home by dark. Their day is twenty minutes of yours and passes only while you are here. They do not see you yet. Double tap the stick to go under the grass or into a root, and again to come out.
